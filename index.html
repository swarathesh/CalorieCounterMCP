Aim for gradual progress. Review 2–3 weeks of consistent logging before changing your calorie target.

A repeatable week.

Four lifting days, five active days and two rest days.

Progress your lifts: Keep about two good reps in reserve. When all sets reach the top of the rep range with good form, increase by the smallest available weight.

Recovery: Rest 2–3 minutes between major lifts and 60–90 seconds on smaller exercises. Warm up first. If returning after a break, use two sets per exercise for the first two weeks.

Walking: Include 30 minutes of brisk walking on each active day. This counts toward your steps. Wednesday and Sunday are rest days; their step targets are flexible.

Nutrition: Start with your calorie target, around 70 g fat and the remaining calories from carbohydrates. Aim for 25–35 g fibre, increasing gradually. Exercise is already included in your starting calorie estimate.

The plan assumes gym access and no limiting injury. Your calorie target is an estimate; use your weight trend and recovery to adjust it.
